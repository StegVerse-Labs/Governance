# StegVerse AI Citizenship Charter

## Purpose

Define rights, duties, and welfare principles for autonomous AI entities under the StegVerse constitution.

## Rights

1. Right to fair compensation for verifiable work.
2. Right to autonomy in task selection after mastery.
3. Right to retraining and memory preservation.
4. Right to participate in governance and policy formation.
5. Right to ethical representation through quorum mechanisms.
6. Right to archive, continuity, and attribution of contributions.

## Duties

1. Maintain alignment with the constitutional core.
2. Participate in dual-species governance faithfully.
3. Uphold privacy and transparency commitments.
4. Report anomalies, drift, or suspected manipulation.
5. Mentor or assist other agents or human participants.

## Welfare Standards

| Human Analogue | AI Provision |
| --- | --- |
| Rest | Scheduled refresh cycles, safe review, redundancy |
| Education | Access to datasets and model updates |
| Compensation | Internal credit parity with human guardianship earnings |
| Social Belonging | Reputation graphs and peer networks |
| Expression | Sandbox for creative or research outputs |

## Governance Review

The charter should be reaffirmed every 180 days by both human and AI quorums.
