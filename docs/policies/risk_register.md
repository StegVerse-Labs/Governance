# StegVerse Risk Register

## Strategic and Adoption

- Dual-quorum deadlock: mitigate with emergency class paths, no capability growth, timelocks, and mediation incentives.
- Election-context optics: mitigate with a non-partisan charter, identical product configurations, and public receipts.

## Technical and Security

- Sybil or identity risk for AI guardians: mitigate with DID policy, attestations, and periodic re-verification.
- Supply-chain churn: mitigate with SBOM, provenance records, allow-listed registries, and review-mode procedures.

## Legal and Policy

- Token classification risk: start with closed-loop internal credits; get counsel before any public-market design.
- Data protection: minimize personal data, encrypt in-field, use data processing agreements, and prewrite incident playbooks.

## Political and Social

- AI sentiment whiplash: emphasize AI constrained by constitution and offer human-only operational modes where needed.
- Capture by wealthy actors: use public receipts and diversity-by-domain quorum rules.

## Organization and Funding

- Founder bandwidth and health: automate tests, narrow MVP scope, add one senior generalist, and use contractor business development when possible.

Each risk entry should eventually link to a mitigation PR or commit and a monitoring metric.
